Dear Customer,

As the creator of FontShelf, I take your privacy seriously. I want to assure you that FontShelf does not collect, store, track, or share any personal information from its users.

Your installed fonts, local folders, custom groups, favorites, custom themes, and any images you use for visual font matching stay 100% locally on your device. No account is required, and your files or images are never uploaded to external servers.

Optional internet access is used solely when you choose to browse, preview, or download fonts from Google Fonts. All other features — including visual font search — work completely offline.

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please don't hesitate to contact me at MTellawy@Gmail.com.
